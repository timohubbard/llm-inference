A great deal of Berkshire's reported gain this year reflects the reduction in federal corporate tax rates enacted in December; that gain is real in an accounting sense, but it does not reflect any operating accomplishment on our part. The underlying performance of our subsidiaries was, however, quite satisfactory. We continue to hold enormous cash reserves, having found few opportunities at prices we are willing to pay. When valuations are high, we are content to wait; we would rather forgo a marginal opportunity than pay a price that leaves no margin of safety.
