Our textile business performed adequately this year, though the returns achieved remain modest in the context of the capital employed. We continue to view the insurance operations as our principal vehicle for long-term gain, and the float generated by those operations — growing steadily alongside the volume we underwrite — provides us with a source of investable funds whose cost, over a cycle, we expect to remain negligible. Opportunities to deploy that capital at prices we find attractive were less plentiful this year than last; we are patient.
