The housing market weakened materially during the year, and the financial institutions whose lending practices fueled the boom are now, predictably, beginning to bear the consequences. It has always been our conviction that those who lend imprudently will, in time, be identified by the tide going out. We are watching with interest, and with some patience, for the assets we would like to own to appear at prices we are willing to pay. Our insurance float continues to grow, providing us with a formidable base of capital from which to act when the opportunity arises.
