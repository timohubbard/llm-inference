Our book value grew at a more modest pace this year than in many recent ones. The marketable securities we hold were priced richly throughout the period, and we declined to add meaningfully to positions whose prospective returns did not compensate us adequately for the capital committed. Diversification, we continue to believe, is a hedge against ignorance — and we prefer to concentrate our capital in the small number of businesses whose economics and management we understand deeply. Patience, too, is capital.
