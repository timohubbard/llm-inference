The decision to close our textile operation was a difficult one, and not one we took lightly. The business has been loyally served for many years by its employees and by its managers, and the losses it has generated in recent years were in no meaningful sense the fault of either. The economics of the industry, however, have deteriorated to a point at which continued operation would have required capital deployment we could not responsibly justify. We thank the employees for their long service and we remain deeply aware of the costs they bear in this transition.
