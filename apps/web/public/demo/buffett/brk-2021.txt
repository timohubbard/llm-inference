The recovery from the pandemic's economic disruption was more rapid than we, or most observers, had anticipated. Our operating subsidiaries, in the aggregate, produced record earnings. We repurchased a meaningful quantity of our own shares during the year, finding them to be, in our judgment, the most attractive long-term investment available to us at prevailing prices. A share repurchase made at a price below intrinsic value is a quiet transfer of wealth from the departing shareholder to those who remain; when made above intrinsic value, the direction reverses.
