Inflation swept through the American economy at a pace not seen in four decades, and the rising cost of capital exerted a predictable and overdue corrective effect on the prices of risky assets. Our operating subsidiaries, as a group, again delivered strong results; the economics of a well-chosen business tend to weather such turbulence in ways that purely financial claims do not. We found little at prices we liked, and our net acquisition activity for the year was accordingly modest. The twin pillars of our approach remain as they have always been: discipline in what we pay, and patience in waiting for the right pitch.
