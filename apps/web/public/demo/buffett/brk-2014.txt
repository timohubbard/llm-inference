This year marks the fiftieth anniversary of Berkshire's current management, and the occasion invites reflection on what has gone right — and on the errors, some of them substantial, that have punctuated the intervening decades. The greatest single factor in whatever success we have achieved has been the extraordinary tailwind provided by the American economic and political system, and our confidence in that tailwind continuing is undiminished. Our operating subsidiaries now earn sums annually that would have seemed implausible even a generation ago. We will continue, as always, to prize simplicity, patience, and the avoidance of unforced errors.
