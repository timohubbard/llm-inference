The attacks of September 11 produced one of the largest insurance losses in history, and General Re bore a meaningful share of that loss. The fault lies with us: we underwrote policies that priced terrorism risk as though it were effectively zero, and we have taken steps to correct that error. Our book value declined this year, the first such decline in many years. We continue to believe in the long-term value of the businesses we own, and we remain unwavering in our commitment to underwriting discipline henceforth.
