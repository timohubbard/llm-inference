Inflation continues to be the principal threat to the economic returns earned by American businesses. Reported earnings, handsome as they appear in a year like this one, overstate the real economic gain once one accounts for the capital that must be reinvested at ever-higher replacement cost merely to maintain productive capacity. Our insurance operations enjoyed a good year in underwriting, and the growth of float remains the single most attractive feature of our present corporate structure. We remain wary of committing capital at the current prices asked for most businesses.
